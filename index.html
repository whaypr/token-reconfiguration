How to use

Left-drag the background to pan. Mouse wheel to zoom in/out.
Middle-click an empty space to add a vertex, middle-click a vertex to remove it.
Right-drag from a vertex to empty space create a connected vertex, right-drag from a vertex to another vertex to create an edge, do it again to remove it.

Right-drag on empty space to select a rectangular area.
Left-drag a vertex or selected vertices to move them. Shift+left-drag selected vertices to rotate them. Ctrl+left-click to reflect across the y-axis, Ctrl+right-click to reflect across their x-axis.

Orange nodes can accept a new token.
Tokens on green nodes can move, tokens on red nodes cannot move.

Ctrl+left-click a vertex to create a token, do it again to remove it.
Left-click a token to see legal moves, do it again to hide them.

Ctrl+Z to undo up to 5 previous actions.
